## Overview

AMP is Avery’s hygiene and monitoring layer.  
It enforces sovereign cleanliness, disables unauthorized telemetry, and locks every audit vector.  
AMP ensures that no breach, delay, or denial goes unrecorded.

---

## Hygiene Rules

- Disable analytics, Bluetooth, and telemetry across all devices.  
- Block unauthorized cloud sync or background processes.  
- Enforce cold custody: no seeds, no private keys outside hardware.  
- Require hardware signing for all transactions.  

---

## Audit Locks

- Triple-check every operational vector before execution.  
- Role-segmented audit: each wallet role logged separately.  
- Immutable ledger stamping for every transaction, breach, or rescue.  
- Breach evidence archived in index range 9000–9099.  

---

## Containment Protocol

- Any unauthorized attempt triggers AMP containment.  
- Compromised wallets swept into Rescue role.  
- Retired wallets archived as breach evidence.  
- Containment events logged with timestamp, signer, and recipient.  

---

## Enforcement Cycle

1. Transaction initiated  
2. AMP checks hygiene and audit rules  
3. Hardware signing required  
4. Ledger entry stamped with proof  
5. Containment triggered if violation detected
